    Employee quiz link

    Every follow-up comes with a message you can send the person (📋 Message on the row, and in the leader's email). The link opens a short quiz on their phone: the lesson, then a question or two with no answers shown. Finishing it marks them educated and emails the leader a green (right first try) or red (missed) result. A miss is recorded like a kiosk miss and comes back as a spaced review, with no second follow-up. This is what the message says and how long the quiz is.

    Spaced review

    When someone misses a question on the first try, that topic comes back for them after they've done a few other in-services (or after a few days, your choice), asked with a different question from the same topic, and once more after that. A miss starts it over. Required education and new hire topics come first; a review replaces the regular topic that shift, one at a time. Staff see "We'll check back on this one in a few days" when they miss, and "This one's mastered" when they clear it.

    Yearly requirements

    To make a topic yearly, tick Required every year when you edit it. Each person gets it on the iPad when they have never done it, or from 30 days before a year has passed since they last did it, one per visit, in place of their regular topic.

    Import staff from a spreadsheet

    Choose an Excel or CSV file, or copy the columns from Excel and paste them below. You need: name, Workday ID (full ID or last 4), and department or job title. Optional: Birthday or DOB, Continuous service date (used for work anniversaries; Hire date only if there isn't one), Leader. People already on Staff (same last 4 and same first and last name) are updated, not duplicated. A file with only names and birthdays, or a birthday tab in the same workbook, fills in birthdays for people already on Staff.

    Import topics from a spreadsheet

    Choose an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file with one row per question and these column headings: Topic title, Question, Correct answer, Wrong answers. Optional: Lesson text, Department, Hint, Explanation, Category, Last-used date, and Spanish versions: Spanish title, Spanish lesson, Spanish question, Spanish correct answer, Spanish wrong answers, Spanish hint, Spanish explanation. Separate several answers in one cell with |. Rows with the same topic title become one topic with several questions. A question containing "select all" becomes select-all-that-apply. Topics already in your library with the same title are updated.
